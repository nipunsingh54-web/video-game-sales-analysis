# Video Game Sales Analysis — Excel

## Sales Performance Analysis Across Games, Genres, Consoles & Regions

This project analyzes video game sales data to understand global sales performance across different years, genres, consoles, and regions.

The analysis uses a dataset of **64,016 game-platform records**, with **18,922 records containing reported total sales**.

## 📊 Project Overview

The analysis focuses on:

- Global video game sales performance
- Sales trends across release years
- Sales by game genre
- Performance of different gaming consoles
- Regional sales distribution
- Relationship between critic scores and sales
- Key sales performance KPIs

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables
- Charts
- Slicers
- Dynamic KPI calculations
- Data analysis and visualization

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

### Global Sales by Year

Analyzed global sales trends across release years to identify changes in video game sales performance over time.

### Sales by Genre

Compared global sales across genres to identify categories with higher overall sales contribution.

Top genres by global sales include:

- Sports
- Action
- Shooter
- Misc
- Racing

### Top 10 Consoles

Compared global sales across gaming platforms to identify consoles with the highest cumulative sales.

### Regional Sales

Analyzed sales across:

- North America
- Japan
- PAL regions
- Other regions

### Critic Score Analysis

Explored critic scores alongside sales performance to understand patterns between game reviews and commercial performance.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand what factors and segments are associated with stronger video game sales performance and provide a structured view of sales trends across platforms, genres, years, and regions.

## 📌 Key Takeaways

- Sports, Action, and Shooter are among the highest-selling genres in the dataset.
- PS2 has the highest cumulative global sales among the listed consoles.
- North America represents the largest regional sales contribution in the dataset.
- The dashboard provides an interactive way to compare sales performance across multiple dimensions.
